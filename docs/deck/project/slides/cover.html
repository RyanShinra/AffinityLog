AffinityLog

I ran eleven antibody design experiments on Amazon Bio Discovery, then built a backend to store and interpret what came out. The interesting part turned out to be that one score column measures three different things.

How the work split

I ran the experiments and made the design decisions: the schema shape, what got built, what got deferred. Claude did most of the typing and knew Postgres better than I did. I wrote the pieces that carry a judgment by hand, including the CASE that classifies each fold and the CSV score parsing.
