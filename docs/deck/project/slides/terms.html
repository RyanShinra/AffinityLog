What the words mean

HER2

A growth-factor receptor on many tissue-cell surfaces. It's overproduced in approximately 20% of breast cancers. Herceptin targets it.

PDB, and 1N8Z

The Protein Data Bank, a public archive of solved 3D structures. 1N8Z is Herceptin bound to HER2.

Antibody, nanobody

A protein that sticks to one target. Made of a heavy and a light chain; a nanobody has heavy only.

Chains: H, L, T

Heavy and light are the antibody's. T is the target, HER2. Which went into a fold is the whole story.

Recipe

Bio Discovery's word for a pipeline. Each module in it writes its own columns into the export.

Boltz2

A folding model. Predicts the 3D shape of the chains together, then scores its confidence in that shape.

ESM2

A language model for proteins. Here it predicts properties from sequence, without folding anything.

EvoProtGrad

Mutates a starting antibody toward better scores, guided by a language model, a step at a time.

BioPhi, Humatch

Humanness scorers. An antibody that doesn't look human can provoke an immune response against the drug.

RFantibody

Designs a new binder from scratch, rather than improving one that already exists.
