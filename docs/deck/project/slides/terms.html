What the words mean

HER2. Human Epidermal growth factor Receptor 2. It sits on the surface of many tissue cells and signals them to grow. Overproduced in approximately 20% of breast cancers, which is what Herceptin targets.

PDB, and 1N8Z. The Protein Data Bank, a public archive of experimentally solved 3D structures. 1N8Z is its entry for Herceptin bound to HER2, and it is the target every experiment here ran against.

Antibody, nanobody. A protein shaped to grip one particular target. It is built from a heavy chain and a light chain. A nanobody is the heavy chain alone, a form first found in camels, and is smaller and easier to make.

Chains: H, L, T. Heavy and Light are the antibody's own two chains. Target is HER2 itself. Which of the three went into a given simulation decides what that run's scores measure, and that is the whole story here.

Recipe. Bio Discovery's word for a pipeline: one or more simulation modules wired in sequence. Each module writes its own columns into the export, which is why the column set changes from run to run.

Boltz2. The folding module. It takes the chain sequences and predicts the 3D shape they fold into together, then scores how confident it is in that shape. Open source, out of MIT, in the AlphaFold family.

ESM2. Evolutionary Scale Modeling, version 2. A language model trained on protein sequences the way an LLM is trained on text. It predicts properties straight from the sequence and folds nothing at all.

EvoProtGrad. Evolutionary Protein Gradients. It mutates a starting antibody one step at a time, letting a language model's gradients point toward the changes likeliest to score better. Directed evolution, in software.

BioPhi, Humatch. Two humanness scoring modules. An antibody that does not read as human enough gets attacked by the patient's immune system as foreign, so these score how human a sequence looks and guide the edits that fix it.

RFantibody. Built on RoseTTAFold, from the Baker lab. It designs a new binder from scratch rather than improving one that already exists. It produced my lowest scoring candidates, which is not a surprise.
