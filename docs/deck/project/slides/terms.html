What the words mean

HER2

A cell-surface protein, overproduced in about one in five breast cancers. Herceptin targets it.

PDB, and 1N8Z

The Protein Data Bank, a public archive of solved structures. 1N8Z is Herceptin bound to HER2.

Antibody, nanobody

A protein that sticks to one target. Heavy chain plus light chain; a nanobody has heavy only.

Chains: H, L, T

Heavy and light are the antibody's. T is the target, HER2 itself. Which got folded is the story.

Recipe

Bio Discovery's word for a pipeline. Each module in it writes its own columns into the export.

Boltz2

A folding model. Predicts the 3D shape of the chains together, and scores its own confidence.

ESM2

A language model for proteins, trained on sequences the way an LLM is trained on text.

EvoProtGrad

Mutates a starting antibody toward better scores, guided by a language model, a step at a time.

BioPhi, Humatch

Humanness scorers. An antibody that doesn't look human draws an immune response.

RFantibody

Designs a new binder from scratch, rather than improving one that already exists.
