For the specialists

The models in the recipes

RFantibody

Writes a sequence

RFdiffusion retrained on antibodies, from the Baker lab. Given HER2 hotspots and a nanobody framework, it designed binders from nothing. Run 1.

EvoProtGrad

Writes a sequence

Directed evolution in software. It proposes point mutations where a protein language model's gradients say the score should rise. Runs 2, 3, 5 and 6.

BioPhi

Humanization step

Sapiens rewrites framework residues toward human antibodies; OASis scores the result against the Observed Antibody Space.

Humatch

Humanization step

A CNN classifier, run as a second opinion. On the same humanized sequence it answered 0.99999 where BioPhi's OASis put it in the 16th percentile.

Boltz2

Predicts the structure

Open source, out of MIT, in the AlphaFold family. It folds whatever chains it is handed into one 3D structure and rates its own confidence. The source of ipTM.

ESM2

Scores a sequence

Used two ways: predicting five properties straight from sequence, and rating how ordinary a sequence looks. Its column says esm2 even when AMPLIFY was the model.

TemStaPro

Scores a sequence

Thermostability from language model embeddings, and the most verbose module here: a raw and a binary call at eight thresholds from 40 to 80 degrees.

FastDPE

Scores a sequence

Paratope charge, CDR hydrophobicity and loop geometry. Run 2 also carried the Nanobody Polyreactivity Scorer, which looks for loops likely to stick to everything.

A ninth, IntelliFold, was added in run 4 and cut the construct below the viable floor; that run produced nothing. Clustering, alignment and liability columns come from built-ins rather than from a module anyone chose.
