For the specialists

The models in the recipes

RFantibody

Designs from scratch

RFdiffusion retrained on antibodies, from the Baker lab. Given HER2 hotspot residues 253 to 283 and a nanobody framework, it generated binders with no starting sequence. Run 1 only.

EvoProtGrad

Evolves a sequence

Directed evolution in software. It proposes point mutations where a protein language model's gradients say the score should rise, and samples which to keep. Runs 2, 3, 5 and 6.

BioPhi

Humanizes

Two tools from Merck. Sapiens rewrites framework residues toward human antibodies, and OASis scores how human the result is against the Observed Antibody Space. Ran on every evolved design.
